# Privacy Policy — Top Rider

_Last updated: 8 September 2026_

This policy explains what the Top Rider app ("the App", "we", "us") collects,
why, and what choices you have. It applies to both riders (drivers) and
customers using the App.

## Information we collect

**You give us:**

- **Phone number** — to create your account and sign you in with a one-time
  code.
- **Name and profile details** — shown to the person you are matched with.
- **Rider documents** — for riders only: photos of your driving licence and
  vehicle papers, and vehicle details (type, number), used to verify you are
  eligible to accept trips.
- **Payment method choice** — e.g. cash or online. Card and UPI details, when
  online payment is used, are handled by our payment processor (Razorpay) and
  are not stored by us.

**Collected automatically:**

- **Location** — while you are using the App: a customer's pickup/drop
  location to request a trip and see the vehicle approaching; a rider's live
  location while they are online, to match nearby trips and share the vehicle's
  position with the customer during a trip. Location is **not** collected when
  the App is closed or when a rider is offline. We do not use background
  location.
- **Device information** — a device identifier and app version, for
  security and to send trip notifications.
- **Trip data** — pickup and drop points, route, distance, time, fare, and
  ratings, kept as a record of the ride.

## How we use it

- Match customers with nearby riders and run the trip.
- Calculate fares and process payments.
- Verify rider identity and eligibility.
- Provide support, resolve disputes, and investigate safety reports.
- Meet legal and tax obligations.

## Sharing

- **Between rider and customer** — first name, vehicle details, approximate
  location, and phone (for the in-app call) are shared with the other party
  for the duration of a trip.
- **Service providers** — hosting, maps (Mapbox), SMS (for the login code),
  and payments (Razorpay), only as needed to run the service.
- **Authorities** — when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Retention

We keep account and trip records for as long as your account is active and
for the period required by law afterward. You can request deletion of your
account (see below).

## Your choices

- **Location permission** can be turned off in your device settings; core
  features of the App will not work without it.
- **Account deletion** — email the address below to have your account and
  personal data deleted, subject to records we must keep by law.

## Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.
