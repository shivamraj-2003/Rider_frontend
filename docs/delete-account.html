Delete your Top Rider account

This page is for users of the Top Rider app (customers and riders). You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Call or send a WhatsApp message to +91 98625 90885 from the phone number registered on your Top Rider account.
  2. Say "Delete my Top Rider account" and tell us whether you are a customer or a rider.
  3. We will confirm your identity using the registered phone number, then delete your account within 7 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number, name and profile details
  • Saved places, emergency contacts and notification settings
  • For riders: uploaded driving licence, vehicle documents and bank/payout details
  • Your push-notification device tokens and any active login sessions

What we keep, and for how long

  • Trip, fare and payment records are kept for up to 8 years as required by Indian tax and accounting law. They are detached from your profile where the law allows, and are used for nothing else.
  • Records needed to investigate a safety report or an open dispute are kept until the matter is closed.

Delete some data only

If you only want specific data removed (for example uploaded documents or saved places) without closing your account, contact us at the same number and tell us what to remove.
